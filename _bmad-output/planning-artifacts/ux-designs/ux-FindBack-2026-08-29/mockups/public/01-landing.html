How FindBack works

1

Report

Tell us what you've lost or found. Add a photo if you can. Reports are visible to other members right away.

2

Match

If you spot something that's yours, submit a claim with a reason and identifying details. The report's owner is notified by email.

3

Return

An administrator reviews each claim, approves the right one, and records the physical handoff. The item status moves to Returned.
