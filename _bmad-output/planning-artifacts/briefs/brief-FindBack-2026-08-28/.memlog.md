- (decision) Q1: FindBack is single-tenant / per-organization. Each deployment serves one organization (school, office, etc.); admins are that org's staff; items stay within the org.
- (decision) Q2: Admin-approved self-registration. New users register themselves and remain Pending until an org administrator approves; only Approved users can use lost-and-found features.
- (decision) Q3: No automatic matching. Users manually browse and search listings; a claim is the human-mediated connection. Search/filter is the sole matching mechanism.
- (decision) Q4: Lifecycle decisions — (a) Returned is admin-only; (b) never-claimed reports are not auto-closed; admin manually closes them. Five-state lifecycle: Open, Claim Requested, Claim Approved, Returned, Closed.
- (decision) Q5: Email-only notifications for exactly three events: (a) account approved, (b) new claim on found item, (c) claim approved/rejected. No in-app notifications; no 'claim under review' reminder; no stale-report reminders.
- (decision) Q6: Claim form contains — required free-text reason, required identifying details, date lost. Contact-channel design deferred to PRD. No proof-of-ownership uploads (receipts, serial numbers).
- (decision) Q7: Edit/delete rules — owner can edit any time until Returned; owner can delete only while status is Open with no claims; once a claim exists, owner withdraws/closes rather than deletes; admin can always edit or remove any report.
- (decision) Q8: Anonymous browsing — summary listings visible without login; item details require login. Balances discoverability with privacy.
- (decision) Q9: Brief carries three design principles — Simple, Trustworthy, Clear. Accessibility and Action-Oriented move to PRD/UX as detailed UX requirements.
- (decision) Q10: Brief carries a short paragraph acknowledging brand/visual identity as a related concern; detailed logo, color palette, and visual specs live in a separate brand/visual spec doc, not the Product Brief.
