- (decision) PRD-author decision (D1): Contact/facilitation between claimant, finder, and administrator. MVP uses in-platform text messages scoped to each claim — a per-claim message thread visible to claimant, finder of the item, and administrators. No phone numbers, email addresses, or external contact methods exposed between members. Administrators mediate by default and may post messages. MVP does not include real-time chat, typing indicators, read receipts, attachments, voice/video, or push notifications related to messages. Email remains the only out-of-platform notification channel for the three locked events.
- (decision) PRD-author decision (D2): Sensitive information and anonymous summary fields. Sensitive fields hidden from non-owners and non-administrators: exact location text (campus/area is shown, specific place text within an org is hidden), reporter's email/phone, claimant's identifying details text, image until owner or admin views detail, receiver-substitution fields. Anonymous summary fields visible without login: item name, category, date, campus/area, status, type (lost/found). Full details (description, image, identifying details, exact location, status history) require login. The reporter and claimant each see additional context appropriate to their role on that item.
- (decision) PRD-author decision (D3): Competing claims UX. All pending claims on a found item remain visible to the administrator in a single review screen, ordered by submission time, with each claim's reason, identifying details, date lost, and claimant information. The administrator approves exactly one claim; all other pending claims on the same item are auto-rejected with reason 'another claim was approved for this item' at the moment the first decision is recorded, and their audit entries are updated accordingly. Rejected claims remain visible in the item history. If two administrators act simultaneously, the first to record an approval wins; the other's submission is presented with a conflict message and requires re-submission.
- (decision) PRD-author decision (D4): Administrator registration-review process. Administrators see a queue of pending registrations with name, email, self-declared role (student/employee/visitor/other) and registration timestamp. Each registration is approved or rejected; rejection requires a short reason that is not exposed to the rejected user but is recorded in the audit trail. Approved accounts are activated immediately. There is no email verification, identity document upload, or background check in MVP. Administrators may bulk-approve only when the queue is empty to prevent accidental bulk action.
